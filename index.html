FAQs

Questions Brantford Homeowners Ask

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

Are your people certified and insured?

Certified, trained, and fully insured on every job, paperwork shown on request. Ontario does not run a trade licence for this work, so those are the credentials that separate a real crew from a rented van, and we bring them.

What does the visit involve and how long does it take?

Main vacuum on, registers washed, each branch brushed while isolated under suction, furnace compartment cleared, photos taken. A typical Brantford home runs three to four hours; big century places with extra returns run longer.

We are mid-renovation in a prewar brick. Is now the time to clean?

Book it for the week the dust settles, literally. Flip and reno work in the older wards puts drywall grit and a century of accumulated debris into motion, and the ducts quietly collect the finest of it. Cleaning after the contractors leave and before the furniture returns is the sequence that makes the rest of the renovation visible.

The furnace smell in October, can anything be done?

Yes. The odour rides on residue left on the duct walls through the idle summer. Cleaning takes the bulk out, and a sanitizer treatment on the clean surfaces handles the remainder.

We cannot keep this house dust-free. Is it us?

Almost certainly not. Joist-panned returns and open joints in older mechanical rooms pull basement air into the system nonstop. Sealing those leaks is the fix, and we can test for them first.

How often should ducts be cleaned in Brantford?

Two to three years handles most homes. River-adjacent properties, houses with pets, and anything recently flipped or renovated should move to the short end. The honest gauge is inside the return boot, which we will show you on arrival.

Do you get outside the city?

Yes, across Brant County, Paris, Burford, St. George, Mount Pleasant, Scotland, and Oakland are all regular stops on our schedule.

Our place sits low near the river. Does valley damp change anything?

It does. The low wards hold humidity straight through summer, and a damp return side holds odour and grows film faster than the dry streets up the hill. Valley homes do best at the short end of the cleaning cycle, with the AC coil washed in the same visit.

Do you serve areas outside of Brantford, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Brantford and nearby communities including Paris, Burford, St. George, Mount Pleasant, and the surrounding area.
